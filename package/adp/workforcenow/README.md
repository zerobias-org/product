# ADP Workforce Now

ADP Workforce Now is a human capital management (HCM) platform from ADP. It combines HR, payroll,
time, benefits and talent management for mid-sized and larger organizations.

## Key capabilities

- **HR management:** Keeps employee records and HR processes in one system.
- **Payroll:** Runs payroll and handles payroll tax filing.
- **Time and attendance:** Tracks employee time and attendance.
- **Benefits administration:** Manages employee benefits enrollment.
- **Talent management:** Supports recruiting, onboarding and performance processes.
