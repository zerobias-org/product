# Paylocity

Paylocity is a cloud-based payroll and human capital management (HCM) platform from Paylocity
Holding Corporation.

## Key capabilities

- **Payroll:** Processes payroll and related tax filings.
- **HR:** Manages employee data, onboarding and HR workflows.
- **Time and benefits:** Handles time tracking and benefits administration.
- **APIs:** Offers developer APIs for integrations.
