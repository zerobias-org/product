# Salesforce

Salesforce is a cloud customer relationship management (CRM) platform from Salesforce, Inc. It
covers sales, service, marketing and commerce applications.

## Key capabilities

- **CRM:** Manages accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities.
- **Customer service:** Handles cases and support workflows.
- **Platform:** Lets teams build custom apps and automations.
- **APIs:** Offers REST, SOAP and other APIs for integrations.
