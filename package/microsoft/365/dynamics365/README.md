# Microsoft Dynamics 365

Microsoft Dynamics 365 is a family of business applications from Microsoft. It covers customer
relationship management (CRM) and enterprise resource planning (ERP).

## Key capabilities

- **Sales and service:** Manages sales pipelines and customer service cases.
- **Finance and operations:** Runs finance, supply chain and other ERP processes.
- **Dataverse:** Stores business data in Microsoft Dataverse.
- **Power Platform:** Extends apps with Power Apps and Power Automate.
