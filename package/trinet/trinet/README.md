# TriNet

TriNet is an HR platform and professional employer organization (PEO) service from TriNet Group,
Inc. It serves small and mid-sized businesses.

## Key capabilities

- **Payroll:** Runs payroll and payroll tax filing.
- **Benefits:** Gives access to employee benefits.
- **HR and compliance:** Supports HR tasks, risk management and compliance.
