# Credly

Credly is a digital credentialing platform from Pearson. Organizations use it to issue and manage
verifiable digital badges.

## Key capabilities

- **Badge issuing:** Issues digital badges for certifications and skills.
- **Verification:** Lets anyone verify a badge and its issuer.
- **Earner profiles:** Lets earners collect and share badges.
- **Analytics:** Reports on badge acceptance and sharing.
