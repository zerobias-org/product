# HubSpot

HubSpot is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform from HubSpot, Inc. It combines
marketing, sales, customer service, content management and operations tools on a shared CRM
database.

## Key capabilities

- **CRM:** Stores contacts, companies, deals and tickets in one shared database.
- **Hubs:** Groups tools into hubs such as Marketing Hub, Sales Hub, Service Hub and Content Hub.
- **Automation:** Automates workflows such as lead routing, email sequences and ticket handling.
- **APIs and integrations:** Offers REST APIs and an app marketplace for connecting other tools.
